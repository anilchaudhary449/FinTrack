## Information stored on your device

FinTrack stores accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, debts, savings goals, preferences, and receipt images locally on your device. The app does not require a FinTrack account or send this ledger to a FinTrack-operated server. Receipts selected for a transaction are copied into app-private storage.

## Network services

When network access is available, FinTrack requests currency reference rates from the Open Exchange Rates API at `open.er-api.com` and caches the latest successful response on the device. Rates are informational estimates, may be delayed, and are not a quote for a bank transfer or currency exchange. If rates are unavailable, values may remain in their original currency rather than reflect a current conversion.

FinTrack includes Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform. These SDKs may process device and advertising identifiers, diagnostics, consent choices, and ad interaction data as described in Google's policies. Ads are only requested after the app's consent flow indicates requests are permitted.

## Sharing and backups

Exports and backups are created on the device. You choose whether to share or upload them through another app or service. Those services handle shared files under their own privacy policies. FinTrack does not receive a copy of files you share.

## Permissions

The app requests notification permission for reminders, biometric capability for optional app unlock, and camera capability for optional receipt capture. Camera access is optional; users can also choose an existing image. The app does not require location, contacts, or microphone access for its core finance features.

## Data retention and deletion

Financial data remains in the app's local database until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Android backups may include app data according to the device's backup settings. Shared exports are retained by the destination app or service you choose.

## Children's privacy

FinTrack is a personal-finance utility and is not designed for children. The publisher must set the intended audience accurately in Play Console and configure advertising treatment appropriately.
